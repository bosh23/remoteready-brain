# ICP and personas

Source: Notion positioning statement (27 Oct 2026). **Personas are hypotheses** (Tulia format). Validate against r/mkd threads, story polls, the question box, early DMs and the first 20 signups.

## Target group
25–40, mostly women (working assumption, check against the first 20 signups), degree-educated, working in or aiming for remote roles with foreign companies. Tech/IT first: highest pay, highest need, clearest buyer. Also outsourcing, customer success, marketing, design, finance/accounting services, and the diaspora.

## Two doors into the same brand
- **Find:** job seekers, people laid off, career switchers going for remote roles. High urgency, tighter budget.
- **Grow:** people already in remote or foreign-company roles who want to sound and be seen as senior. Budget, lower urgency, need a trigger.

Plus the **diaspora** (both doors) and **B2B** (HR/People buying for teams or for people leaving).

**How they find us:** Instagram (organic + light paid), friends and colleagues sharing posts, Dijana's LinkedIn, diaspora Facebook groups, the email list, later search and local press.

## Persona 1: Ana, 31, Skopje – on mute (Grow)
- **Who:** senior QA / PM / developer at an outsourcing company with a US or German client. Salary well above the MK average.
- **A typical day:** standup with the client team at 10, Slack all day, a weekly demo. Writes fine (ChatGPT helps), understands everything. On calls she builds the sentence in her head and the moment passes.
- **Biggest problem:** sounds more junior than she is. Gets passed over for client-facing and lead roles.
- **How we solve it:** Speak Ready now, Senior Ready next.
- **Trigger moments:** right after a call where she stayed quiet · performance review · offered a lead role · new client kickoff · feedback to "be more visible".
- **Where she spends time:** Instagram in the evening, LinkedIn passively, Viber/WhatsApp with colleagues.
- **Objections:** "My English isn't that bad" · no time after work · "I'll just watch YouTube."

## Persona 2: Elena, 34, Skopje – between jobs (Find)
- **Who:** laid off from a tech company in 2026 (customer success, marketing, or PM). Going for remote roles with foreign companies. Severance covers a few months.
- **A typical day:** job boards, applications, rewriting the CV, second-guessing her LinkedIn.
- **Biggest problem:** doesn't know where remote roles actually get filled, gets few recruiter replies, blanks in English interviews.
- **How we solve it:** Job Ready, LinkedIn Ready, Interview Ready. Speak Ready if speaking is the real gap.
- **Trigger moments:** the layoff · first rejection · an interview invite for next week · the January job-search push.
- **Where she spends time:** LinkedIn (very active now), Instagram, job-seeker and tech Facebook groups.
- **Objections:** budget while unemployed · "I can do this myself with ChatGPT." Answer: yes, you can. We show you how.

## Persona 3: Sara, 29, Munich – the diaspora professional (Grow)
- **Who:** moved abroad 5 years ago, works in English at an international company, earns in EUR.
- **Biggest problem:** fluent enough, but less sharp than native colleagues. Small talk, tone, pushing back. Feels her personality gets lost in English, and it shows in how senior she's seen.
- **How we solve it:** Speak Ready (the nuance explained in Macedonian), then Senior Ready.
- **Trigger moments:** new manager · promotion conversation · feedback about "communication" · switching jobs.
- **Where she spends time:** Instagram, Macedonian diaspora Facebook groups.
- **Objections:** time zones (evening CET works) · "Is this too basic for me?"

## Persona 4: Biljana, 38 – the HR buyer (decides or recommends, doesn't attend)
- **Who:** People / L&D manager at a Skopje IT company, 100–300 people, mostly foreign clients.
- **Biggest problem:** client feedback about communication, strong engineers who stay quiet on calls, new leads who don't act senior yet. Needs a clear outcome, an invoice, a certificate, a schedule around delivery.
- **Trigger moments:** Q4 budget planning · a client complaint · a new US client · promotions to team lead · layoffs (outplacement for people leaving).
- **Buys:** team programs, team workshops, outplacement.
- **Also in this role:** the team lead, friend or partner who sends our post to Ana or Elena. Make content easy to forward.

## When should people think of Remote Ready?
- The day they're laid off, or decide to look for a remote role
- When recruiters don't reply
- The week before an interview in English
- After a meeting where they stayed quiet
- Before a performance review or promotion conversation
- New client, new manager, new team
- January goal-setting
- For HR: Q4 budget planning, a client complaint, a round of layoffs or promotions
