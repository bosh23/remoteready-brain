# Marketing strategy

Source: Notion positioning statement (27 Oct 2026).

**Goal:** fill each workshop and cohort, grow the email list (the asset we own), and build a B2B pipeline. Every activity should move at least one of those three.

## Principles
- Trust first, then sell. Go live without an offer; sell once the audience knows us and has told us what it needs. Then: one offer per push; everything else waits.
- Owned over rented: every channel points to the email list or a waitlist.
- Not Instagram-first. One message, adapted per channel; show up where each persona is at their trigger moment.
- Content solves an immediate problem: the meeting tomorrow, the interview next week, the profile nobody views.
- Proof over promises. No invented numbers.
- Dijana shows up where trust is needed; the brand carries the rest.

## Funnel
1. **Reach:** useful content and conversations where the personas already are.
2. **Capture:** a lead magnet or waitlist per line (see `offers-pricing.md`).
3. **Nurture:** welcome sequence and a regular newsletter. Anonymous questions get answered in content.
4. **Convert:** a free live session per push, then a time-boxed sales window (founding prices, real deadline), personal DMs, B2B calls.
5. **Loop:** testimonials, before/after examples, alumni referrals, alumni moving on to the next line.

## Content pillars
Channel-neutral and mapped to the path.
1. **Speak up** (Grow · Speak Ready): say this, not that; meetings, calls, standups, pushback, small talk, tone.
2. **Get found** (Find · LinkedIn Ready, Job Ready): LinkedIn in English, where remote roles get posted, applications, what recruiters look for.
3. **Get the offer** (Get · Interview Ready): interview questions, telling your story, salary and offer conversations.
4. **Be seen as senior** (Grow · Senior Ready): visibility, stakeholder communication, leading meetings, promotions.
5. **Remote work, honestly** (context): the remote job market, layoffs, what foreign companies expect, life in international teams, the diaspora at work. Always sourced, never invented.
6. **Proof** (all lines): participant wins, before/after, behind the scenes.

**Weighting:** during a push, about half the content comes from that line's pillar; the rest rotates. Before the first offer, rotate all pillars and track saves, shares, replies and ad sign-ups.

**Rhythm per push (3–4 weeks):** warm-up content → lead magnet → free live session → sales window with a deadline → close → proof content.

## Content starter ideas (EN; Dijana writes the MKD)
- **Speak up:** ChatGPT can write your email. In the meeting, you're on mute. · 5 phrases that make you sound junior on a client call, and what to say instead · How to say "no" to a client politely in English · A 30-second standup update that sounds senior
- **Get found:** Your LinkedIn headline in English: 3 mistakes recruiters scroll past · Where remote roles actually get posted (and where they don't)
- **Get the offer:** Remote job interview: 3 questions that catch people off guard · "Tell me about yourself" in 60 seconds
- **Be seen as senior:** The difference between doing senior work and being seen as senior
- **Remote work, honestly:** Living abroad for years and still hesitating before every email?
- **Proof:** a participant's before/after LinkedIn headline or standup update (with permission)

## What we measure
Email subscribers, lead magnet downloads, live session attendance, signups by source, conversation → sale rate, cost per lead on boosts, repeat buyers across lines. Followers and reach are secondary.
