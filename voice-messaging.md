# Voice and messaging

Source: Notion positioning statement (27 Oct 2026) + remoteready-site CLAUDE.md.

## Voice
Direct, warm, specific. A sharp colleague who's been there. Examples over promises. The brand speaks as "we"; Dijana speaks as herself on camera. Not a teacher, coach or guru.

- Public copy in Macedonian (Cyrillic). English work terms (remote, LinkedIn, CV) can stay in Latin script.
- No emoji in headings or design elements.
- Host intro line (use as is): "Здраво, јас сум Дијана. Последниве 10 години работам на глобален пазар и ќе те научам како да го направиш истото."

## Messaging pillars
1. **Your skills aren't the problem. How you show up is.** On LinkedIn, in interviews, in meetings, in English. (Umbrella message.)
2. **ChatGPT can write your email. It can't unmute you in the meeting.** (Lead hook for Speak Ready.)
3. **Smart ≠ seen.** If recruiters and managers don't see your value, it doesn't count. (LinkedIn Ready, Job Ready, Senior Ready.)
4. **You can do this yourself. We show you how.** (The no-coach approach.)

## Words we use
remote role, work English, client call, meeting, interview, LinkedIn, job search, sound confident, sound senior, practical, small group, toolkit, next step.

## Words we avoid
perfect English, fluent in 30 days, dream job guaranteed, "English course" (puts us next to language schools), coach-speak ("unlock your potential"), hustle, girlboss tone, fear-mongering about layoffs.

## Proof
Before/after phrases and profiles, participant wins (with permission), real screenshots. **Never fabricated numbers or testimonials.**

## Visuals
- Clean and premium, one strong color + neutrals, text-led carousels, before/after screenshots (Slack, email, LinkedIn headlines), Dijana on camera as the host.
- Instagram design system: stickers, highlight pills, ticker bands with program names, loopy arrows. Playful, not plain or dated.
- Photos of Dijana: background-removed cutouts (striped-shirt shots). Don't make her face the center or half the image.
- Carousel host signature: avatar circle + "Дијана" / "Remote Ready" on two lines, same on every slide.
- Carousel CTA: "Прати го ова на некој што бара remote работа", secondary "Дознај повеќе → remoteready.mk".
- Visually separate from Dijana's personal brand (i am dijana). Don't copy the Remote Ready app's visuals.
