# Launch plan

Source: Notion "Remote Ready: Launch Plan" (27 Oct 2026). Action items live in the Notion Action Items database, not here.

**Rules (27 Oct):** go live and build trust first, then sell. No offer at go-live. The first offer is picked from what the audience responds to (r/mkd threads, story polls, DMs, ad tests). Program materials get built only after seats are sold.

## Launch sequence (stages, not dates)

| Stage | What happens | Moves on when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Go live | Instagram, the LinkedIn page and the remoteready.mk sign-up page go public. No offer. Warm list gets a personal heads-up. | Everything is public and the warm list has been messaged |
| 2. Build trust | Posts across all pillars, value posts in diaspora and job-seeker Facebook groups, an ad test by topic, listening on r/mkd, story polls and the question box. No sales. | Decision signal reached |
| 3. Pick the first offer | Story poll + ad test + DMs decide what people need first. Build the matching lead magnet and send it to the list. | First offer and lead magnet chosen |
| 4. Test, then sell | Soft-test offer and price with 5 people who match the personas, free live session, time-boxed sales window with founding prices. | First seats sold |
| 5. Deliver and repeat | Run it, collect testimonials, pick the next line from what participants ask for. | — |

**Decision signal (planning assumption, not a benchmark):** about 100 email subscribers or 6 weeks after go-live, whichever comes first.

**Ad test:** one ad per problem (LinkedIn/visibility, English in meetings, finding a remote job), same budget, same sign-up page. Compare cost per sign-up between ads. Cookie consent undercounts every ad, so compare them to each other, not to benchmarks.

**B2B:** separate track. Warm conversations with HR/L&D contacts can start anytime.

## Growth phases
- **Phase 1 – Build trust, then validate:** go live · ~100 subscribers · first offer picked · 5-person soft test · first seats sold · 3 B2B conversations · 5 testimonials · ~€2k/month once a paid line runs regularly.
- **Phase 2 – Repeat and add (Apr–Jun 2027):** add Interview Ready and/or Job Ready · new Speak Ready cohort every month · monthly Saturday workshop · first repeat buyers · 500 subscribers · first B2B deal · ~€3k/month.
- **Phase 3 – Scale (Jul–Dec 2027):** Senior Ready for alumni · B2B program at least every 2 months · second trainer if there's a waitlist · self-paced toolkits · decide on membership · local press · ~€5k/month.

## Income math
Assumption: ~20% set aside for tax, tools and payment fees (verify with an accountant). Take-home €2k / €3k / €5k needs revenue of €2,500 / €3,750 / €6,250.

Units: full Speak Ready cohort = 8 × €450 = €3,600 · full Saturday workshop = 15 × €119 = €1,785 · a running cohort ≈ 14h/month · a workshop ≈ 8h.

| Target | Mix (monthly average) | Revenue | Take-home | Delivery | Buyers / month |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| €2k | New cohort every 2 months + workshop every 2 months | €2,693 | ~€2,150 | ~18h | ~12 |
| €3k | New cohort every month (2 running) + workshop every 2 months | €4,493 | ~€3,590 | ~32h | ~16 |
| €5k | New cohort every month + workshop every month + B2B team program every 2 months | €7,635 | ~€6,110 | ~50h | 23 + 1 company every 2 months |
| €5k (B2C only) | New cohort every month + 2 workshops a month | €7,170 | ~€5,740 | ~44h | 38 |

**Honest read:**
- These assume full seats. Empty seats are normal early on.
- €2k is realistic in phase 1. €3k needs a monthly cohort rhythm. €5k is B2B-led; B2C-only means ~38 buyers a month from a small market.
- Trust-first cost: no revenue until the first offer sells (~2–3 months from go-live if the 6-week signal holds).
- Planning assumption: roughly 1 in 10 real conversations buys. 8 seats ≈ 80 conversations. Replace with real numbers after the founding cohort.
- Capacity: past ~40h/month of delivery, bring in a second trainer. Not before there's a waitlist.
