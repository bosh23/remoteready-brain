# Offers and pricing

Source: Notion positioning statement + launch plan (27 Oct 2026).

## The Remote Ready path

| Stage | Line | What it covers | Format | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Find | **Job Ready** | Where remote roles are posted and filled, targeting, CV, applications, outreach, running a job search like a project | Saturday workshop or short group sprint (validate first) | Phase 2 |
| Find | **LinkedIn Ready** | Profile in English, headline and About, visibility, getting found by recruiters, outreach | Saturday workshop, €119 (early bird €99) | Candidate for the first offer (picked after the trust phase) |
| Get | **Interview Ready** | Interviews in English, telling your story, tough questions, salary and offer conversations | Saturday workshop | Phase 2 |
| Grow | **Speak Ready** | Advanced English and communication: meetings, calls, standups, pushback, small talk, writing | 8 weeks, 2 live group calls a week, max 8, €450 (founding cohort €390) | Core program; candidate for the first offer |
| Grow | **Senior Ready** | Being seen as senior: visibility, stakeholder communication, leading meetings, self-advocacy, promotion and raise conversations | Program or workshop series (validate first) | Phase 3, alumni first |
| All | **B2B** | Team programs (Speak Ready, Senior Ready for new leads) · outplacement (Job + LinkedIn + Interview Ready for people leaving) · one-day team workshops | €4,500 team program · €1,500 workshop | Conversations from Oct 2026 |

- **Speak Ready descriptor:** advanced English for meetings, calls and everyday work. Entry level B1/B2 and up: you already speak English, but you freeze, over-prepare, or sound more junior than you are.
- **LinkedIn Ready vs. Job Ready (decided):** LinkedIn Ready is the profile and visibility workshop. Job Ready (search strategy) is a separate, later line.
- **Formats, entry to scale:** free (lead magnets, monthly live session) → Saturday workshops → multi-week programs → B2B. Later: self-paced toolkits, possibly a membership. **No 1:1 product.**
- **Wide promise, narrow launch:** the brand promises the whole path; we launch one line at a time. Go live without an offer, build trust, pick the first line from what the audience responds to. Materials get built only after seats are sold. Senior Ready comes last, when alumni ask for it.
- **Scholarship seats:** 1–2 reduced-price places per cohort or workshop for people laid off in 2026.
- **Cohort size:** 8 by default (speaking time). 10 is possible with breakout rooms (+€900 per cohort).

## Lead magnets (one per line)
- Speak Ready: phrase bank for meetings
- LinkedIn Ready: LinkedIn profile checklist
- Job Ready: where remote roles get posted
- Interview Ready: interview question bank

## Business model
- **Revenue streams:** workshops (entry), programs (core), B2B (scale). Later: toolkits, possibly a membership.
- **Price anchor:** a premium business English course in MK charges €400 for 12 weeks.
- **Why the one-stop shop scales:** one person can move through several lines, and one company can buy several. Illustrative: LinkedIn Ready + Interview Ready + Speak Ready = €688 from one person over about 6 months, versus €450 from an English-only brand.
- **Invoicing (decided 27 Oct):** invoice as an individual, pay personal income tax at UJP. Register a company once revenue justifies it.
