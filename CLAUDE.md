# Remote Ready – brain

The strategy behind Remote Ready (remoteready.mk): positioning, personas, offers, voice, marketing strategy and launch plan. Private. Nothing here is published.

Use this repo as the source of truth for any Remote Ready work: posts, carousels, emails, landing page copy, ads, B2B one-pagers. The website code lives in a separate repo (`bosh23/remoteready-site`); never copy strategy files into it, because everything in the site repo is public.

## Files
- `positioning.md` – who we are, why we exist, mission/values, the no-coach approach, why us, competitors.
- `icp-personas.md` – target group, the two doors (Find / Grow), personas Ana, Elena, Sara, Biljana, trigger moments.
- `offers-pricing.md` – the Remote Ready path (lines, formats, prices, status) and the business model.
- `voice-messaging.md` – voice, messaging pillars, words we use/avoid, proof rules, visuals.
- `marketing-strategy.md` – principles, funnel, channels, content pillars, rhythm per push, what we measure.
- `launch-plan.md` – stages, decision signal, growth phases, income math, open flags.
- `decisions.md` – dated log of positioning decisions. Add a line whenever something changes.
- `data/`, `dashboards/` – raw metric exports (by date) and generated dashboards. `data/log.csv` is the running trend.

## Where things live
- **This repo:** strategy that changes rarely. Edit here first.
- **Notion:** day-to-day execution – Action Items database, Remote Ready Content Calendar, drafts. Don't duplicate those here.
- When a positioning page in Notion and a file here disagree, ask me which is current. Don't merge silently.

## Rules for any output
- Public copy is in Macedonian (Cyrillic). I write or approve the final Macedonian. Drafts in English are fine when I ask.
- Follow `voice-messaging.md`. No "perfect English", "fluent in 30 days", "dream job", coach-speak, hustle tone, or fear-mongering about layoffs.
- Never invent numbers, testimonials or participant results.
- Never name my former employers in public content ("international teams I've worked with", "a previous company").
- Personas are hypotheses. When real signals come in (signups, DMs, polls, ad tests), propose updates to `icp-personas.md` and log them in `decisions.md`.
- Commit messages in English, short.
