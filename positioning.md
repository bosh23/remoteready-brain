# Positioning

Source: Notion "Remote Ready: Founding & Positioning Statement" (last edited 27 Oct 2026). Repositioned 30 Sep 2026: Remote Ready is a one-stop shop for finding and growing in remote roles, not an English-classes brand. English and communication is one line of several.

## Who we are
Remote Ready is a Macedonian career brand for people who want to find and grow in remote roles. That means working for or with foreign companies and teams, from home or from a local office. We cover the whole path in one place: finding the job, LinkedIn, interviews, English and communication at work, and the step up to senior. Taught in Macedonian, delivered as group programs and workshops.

- **Descriptor:** Everything you need to find and grow in a remote role. In Macedonian.
- **Category:** remote career readiness. Not an English school, not a generic career coach.
- **Name / domain:** Remote Ready · remoteready.mk

## Why we exist
- **Remote roles are open to candidates from everywhere.** People from Macedonia have the skills. What holds them back is how they show up: on LinkedIn, in applications, in interviews, in meetings, in English.
- **The help that exists is fragmented.** Language schools teach general English, English coaches teach speaking, career coaches fix CVs. Nobody connects it to the actual goal: getting the remote role and growing in it.
- **Layoffs made it urgent.** More people are looking, and remote hiring is competitive.
- **AI helps with writing.** It doesn't speak for you on the call, and it doesn't make you visible.

## Mission, vision, values
- **Mission:** help Macedonian professionals find, land and grow in remote roles, with English, visibility and confidence that match their skills.
- **Vision:** Macedonian professionals compete for any role, with any team, anywhere, and how they show up is never the reason they lose.
- **Values:** practical over theoretical · self-sufficiency over dependency · honesty (no "fluent in 30 days", no "dream job in 2 weeks") · small groups, real practice · respect for what people already know.

## The no-coach approach
You don't need a coach forever. You need the playbook and enough practice to use it on your own. We show you how, you do the reps, and you leave every program with a toolkit you keep. No 1:1 dependency, no endless lessons.

## The story we tell
The hero is the professional who's great at her job but isn't getting the role, the promotion, or the voice in the meeting she's capable of. Remote Ready is the guide. We hand her the playbook for each step (the profile, the search, the interview, the meeting, the step up), and she does it herself.

## First impression and brand experience
- **What people should think the first time they see us:** "This is exactly where I'm stuck." And: "These people know how remote work actually works."
- **Experience:** premium, calm, organized. Clear schedules, materials on time, real practice from minute one, a toolkit that's useful after the program ends. One place for the whole path: finish one program and you know what comes next. Tone of a sharp colleague, not a school and not a guru.

## Presence
Brand-first, not faceless. Dijana appears in photos, videos and live sessions as the host, but the brand isn't built on her name (not "English with Dijana"). She shows up where trust is needed: intro video, landing page, live sessions, testimonials, PR.

## Why us
Led by Dijana: university degree in English, 10+ years building a career in B2C and B2B SaaS with international companies, working in English every day with teams, clients and leadership, and a marketing background (positioning, LinkedIn, how people get noticed). She teaches the path she's walked. Never name former employers in public content.

## Competitors and how we're different
- **English coaches** (several in MK): speaking and confidence only. That's one step of the path, and where we'd collide if we only did English.
- **Language schools and premium business English courses** (e.g. €400 for 12 weeks): general, long, not tied to getting or growing in a role.
- **Career coaches and CV services:** documents and 1:1 advice, rarely remote-specific, rarely in Macedonian.
- **Job boards, recruiters, AI tools:** listings and text, not skills.

**Why people choose Remote Ready:**
1. The whole remote-career path in one place: find, get, grow
2. In Macedonian, so the nuance lands
3. Built for real moments: the profile, the application, the interview, the meeting, the promotion
4. Small groups, real practice
5. You leave with a toolkit and don't need us forever
6. Led by someone who built an international career in English, with a degree in English and a marketing background

## Beyond Smart → Remote Ready
Beyond Smart ("Marketing for your career") is absorbed. LinkedIn Ready and Senior Ready carry its content: LinkedIn, CV, storytelling, self-advocacy, visibility. What carries over: the "Smart ≠ Seen" pillar, scripts and frameworks over fluff, the email list as the main asset, and anonymous Q&A for community.
